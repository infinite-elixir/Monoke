# T8.15 — Add left/right tap zones to the viewer

## Goal

Provide a simple tap navigation affordance for users who do not swipe. A
stationary tap in the inset left or right edge zone must use the existing
horizontal navigation dispatcher, including post-boundary behavior.

## Interaction contract

- The outer left and right portions of the viewer stage are logical tap zones.
- A safe inset keeps taps near the close/fullscreen chrome out of navigation.
- The centre of the stage remains inert.
- Only a short, stationary pointer interaction counts as a tap; a drag remains
  swipe-only.
- Taps on video/audio, links, buttons, the hint, toast, flash, or explicitly
  protected elements never navigate.
- Left invokes `prev-image`; right invokes `next-image`, including the existing
  cross-post edge behavior.
- Bookmark mode uses the same dispatcher and therefore retains its existing
  post-navigation restrictions.
- Tap navigation has no additional feedback beyond the existing viewer state,
  announcement, and hint dismissal.

## Scope

- [ ] Add constants and hit testing for inset left/right stage zones.
- [ ] Distinguish stationary taps from T8.12 pointer drags without changing
      swipe thresholds, axis locking, or layer behavior.
- [ ] Suppress the synthetic click after a zone tap so one interaction cannot
      produce a second action.
- [ ] Preserve control/media exclusions and cleanup behavior.
- [ ] Add focused static/harness coverage.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A stationary tap in the left zone calls `prev-image`.
- [ ] A stationary tap in the right zone calls `next-image`.
- [ ] A centre tap does not navigate.
- [ ] A tap near the excluded chrome inset does not navigate.
- [ ] A drag still follows and commits through the existing swipe path only.
- [ ] Taps on video/native controls and viewer buttons remain usable.
- [ ] At horizontal post boundaries, taps use the same safe cross-post path as
      keyboard, wheel, and swipe navigation.
- [ ] No extra viewer media layers or persistent overlay DOM are introduced.

## Out of scope

- Vertical tap zones.
- New visible buttons, icons, or toolbar controls.
- Double-tap zoom, long-press menus, or tap feedback animation.
- Changes to keyboard, wheel, or swipe direction semantics.
